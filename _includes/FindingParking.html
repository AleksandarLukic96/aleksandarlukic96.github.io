So, How Do You Find Parking?

There is no easy answer to this question. The parking situation in Copenhagen is complicated. Not just for the people trying to find parking, but also for the ones responsible for solving the infrastructural problems. Numerous actors are trying in their own way to make getting around the city easier.

If you are simply trying to concretely figure out where to find parking in Copenhagen, we suggest using the appropriate tools for that, such as the app EasyPark or the government website kbhkort.kk.dk. You can also look at the city overview map right below here, where all the street parking spaces are shown along how occupied the crrosponding district is throughout the day.

If we simply look at the overall data of car ownership versus street parking, the future is looking dim. Our research, at the very least, points to a trend where street parking not is not a viable bet when trying to find parking.

In our research, we have not touched upon the public transportation which is obviously also a big part of the potential solution. For a moment, consider a Copenhagen where public transportation is so established that private parking becomes irrelevant. Of course that scenario is still well into the future, so until then we recommend planning your trip. Consider where you are going and if there is likely to be parking there. Use our or others' ressources to predict the occupancy in the area and if the odds are against you: use public transportation. The above overview should help you get a better idea of the parking situation in the city and predict the chances of finding parking.

And if none of this works, buy yourself an eletric car and skip some of the hassle.
