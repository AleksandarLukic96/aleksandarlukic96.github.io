Parking In Copenhagen

Talk to any car owner in Copenhagen and they will talk your ear off about how difficult it is to park anywhere in Copenhagen, unless you own your own parking spot. Everyone from news outlets to committees and private persons have all been raising their voices for years that the parking situation is getting worse [Berlingske] [Vanløse Lokaludvalg] [TV2].

Is this the reality or just complaints and bad planning? To answer that question, we need to look at the parking situation in Copenhagen and how it has changed over the years. Parking has always been a hot topic in Copenhagen, and it has been a source of frustration for many residents and visitors alike. The city has a long history of car ownership, and as the number of cars on the road has increased, so too has the demand for parking spaces. To explore the parking habits of inhabitants in Copenhagen, we will look at the parking spaces available in the city and the number of cars registered in the cities' districts and try to show and explain the evolution of parking in the city over the years. The data for parking spaces can be found at opendata.dk and occupancy can be found on opendata.dk as well. The data car ownership can be found at kk.statistikbank.dk, and we derived the districts from another dataset at opendata.dk.
